GUI widgets are built on plain gpui, in `crates/ossteam-gui/src/widgets/`.
Do not add `gpui-component`. The gpui examples at the pinned rev (in
Cargo.toml) are the reference:
`$CARGO_HOME/git/checkouts/zed-*/<rev>/crates/gpui/examples`.
